# Gold Guy

I build free tools that help people buy and sell precious metals for less.

## Projects

### [Bullion Price Check](https://bullionpricecheck.com)
Compares bullion prices from US dealers, checked about hourly. Gold, silver and platinum coins and bars are ranked by lowest premium over spot, along with the live gold spot price.
- [Lowest premiums](https://bullionpricecheck.com/lowest-premium/)
- [Compare dealers](https://bullionpricecheck.com/compare/)
- [Free price widget and API](https://bullionpricecheck.com/developers/)

### [OunceScout](https://ouncescout.com)
Shows live gold, silver, platinum and palladium spot prices, the gold-to-silver ratio, and delivered bullion prices across 23+ verified US dealers.
